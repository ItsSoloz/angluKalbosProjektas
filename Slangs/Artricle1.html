Is youth slang just a joke, or a real language?

Mantas Volkavičius 2026-11-11

Introduction

Have you ever listened to teenagers and felt lost? Words like “rizz” or “skibidi” can sound like another language, even if you speak English well. I chose this topic because slang is a big part of youth culture. In my opinion, slang is not only funny, but also a useful way to understand how young people think and communicate.

Personality and Social Image

Firstly, many slang words describe personality and social image. Aura means your general “coolness”, so doing something impressive gives you “+1,000 aura”, while embarrassing yourself takes it away. People who try too hard to look cool are “aura farming”. Rizz is short for “charisma” and means charm, especially when flirting. Smooth game is “W rizz”, and failing badly is “L rizz”. Another word is sigma, which describes a cool, “lone wolf”. It started in serious self-improvement communities, but teenagers now use it mostly as a joke, for example: “That was so sigma.”

In addition, some slang words are popular just because they are funny. 6-7 is linked to the height of a basketball player, 6 feet 7 inches, and it became famous through viral videos. Young people shout it with hand movements, often to confuse adults, but it has no real meaning. Skibidi comes from the YouTube series Skibidi Toilet. It can mean a lot of things: “cool”, “bad”, “weird” or nothing at all, so it works as an ironic filler word. For example, friends might say “skibidi” to make everyone laugh.

Slang in Everyday Situations

However, slang is also used in everyday situations. If you are cooked, you are in big trouble, for example when you did not study for a test. But when someone says “let him cook”, it means “let him do his thing, he knows what he is doing”. Moreover, mogging means looking better than someone else just by standing next to them, as in “He completely mogged everyone in that group photo”. Finally, glazing means giving too much attention, so if a friend says your bad joke is the funniest ever, they are probably glazing you.

Conclusion

To conclude, learning slang is useful for several reasons. It helps you understand English as people really speak it, not only as it appears in textbooks. It also helps you follow social media, videos and memes, and it helps parents and teachers understand young people better.
