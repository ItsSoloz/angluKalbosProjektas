Is the youth being reckless or strategic about how they plan for the future?

Ugnius Čiužinskas 2026-11-12

Nowadays, teenagers dream of greater things than living day to day, doing what they're told to do and accepting everything as it is thrown at them. Most of the older generations may see this as reckless disobedience with no pay off, but for teenagers it is truly well worth the risks it imposes. Teenagers actively seek higher ambitions rather than being average and it shows with their plans for tomorrow. Many of them want to chase careers tied to their hobbies or passions, seeking work for more than just making a living. Many young people desire more than those who came before them.

A large amount of young people wish to prepare for the future the best they can, often times doing things that are seen by others as wasting time, like building online communities, learning digital skills or doing community service. But to them it's much more than doing something just for the sake of doing it. Be it helping others with minimal tasks or doing far more ambitious jobs, they carve the path to their dreams one way or another.

Furthermore while the youth strongly desire personal success they are also equally invested in building a better future for everyone. Regardless of how grim or bleak the future seems to become, young people are still trying to push through all of it in order to enjoy life, not recklessly or selfishly doing things that only benefit themselves. In the end, young people are having a tug of war with the future and they intend on winning with a combined effort and shared desire to refuse things the way they are.
