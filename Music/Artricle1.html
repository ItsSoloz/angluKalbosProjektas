Can Music and Hobbies Really Shape Who We Are?

Arnas Molis 2026-11-12

Introduction

Music and personal hobbies are a very important part of youth life today. Almost every young person spends a lot of time listening to songs or doing their favorite free time activities. Some people think these things are only for fun while others see a deeper meaning. In my opinion music and hobbies are necessary for youth because they help young people relax and understand themselves better.

The Role of Music in Youth Life

Firstly, youth listen to many different kinds of music from pop and hip hop to rock and indie songs. For example, putting on headphones on the way home from school helps students forget about difficult lessons and tests. Moreover music allows young people to deal with their feelings when they are sad or tired. Talking about favorite artists or going to small concerts with friends also makes youth feel closer to each other and less alone.

Discovering Talents Through Hobbies

Secondly, having a hobby gives young people a great chance to rest and discover their talents. For instance, drawing, playing sports, taking photos, or learning a musical instrument helps youth take a break from their phones and schoolwork. Even when a hobby feels challenging at first seeing small progress brings real joy and shows young people what they are truly good at.

Active Hobbies vs. Passive Scrolling

However, some youth spend too much time on passive activities like scrolling on social media or just lying in bed with headphones all day. To avoid this problem schools and parents could offer more interesting clubs and creative spaces where young people can try new things. When youth only watch others online instead of trying something themselves, it becomes much harder for them to find what really makes them happy.

Conclusion

To conclude, I believe that music and hobbies play a huge role in helping youth relax and grow as individuals. Young people should keep exploring their interests while adults should support their creative choices. Only with this balance can youth enjoy their early years and discover who they really want to be.
